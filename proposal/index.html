Good news

Your reviews are among the best in Norman.

263 reviews at 4.8 for Norman and 169 at 4.8 for Moore, with new ones as recently as late August. Patients name your budtenders and the service. That reputation is the hard part, and you already have it.

Website

The domain works; the site behind it expired.

howhighdispensary.com is registered at GoDaddy and renewed through 16 June 2028. It still points to a Squarespace account that has expired, so every visitor sees “Website Expired”. The Internet Archive shows it working in 2022, and it has been down since at least October 2023. When it did work, the page title was “Wilson Medical”, left over from a template, and it listed only Moore.

Google

Norman’s listing has no website; Moore’s is filed as a “Wellness Center”.

Anyone who finds Norman on Google Maps has nowhere to click through. Moore sends people to Instagram, which shows nothing to anyone who isn’t logged in.

Menu

No menu for Norman anywhere online.

We found none on Leafly, Weedmaps, Dutchie or Jane. Leafly lists Moore only, with no menu, and a Weedmaps listing that existed has been taken down. Patients who shop by menu choose someone else.

Search

Open until 10–11 PM, but missing from “open late”.

For “dispensary open late Norman OK” Google shows High Society, King Kanna and Buddies, not How High. You rank well near OU (#2), on the west side (#3) and in 73069 (#4), but only #9 to #12 for plain “dispensary Norman”.
