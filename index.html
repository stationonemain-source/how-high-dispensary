Questions

Good to know

What do I need to bring?

Your Oklahoma medical marijuana patient or caregiver license and a photo ID.

Do you take cards?

Yes. Both stores take debit cards as well as cash.

How late are you open?

Norman: until 10 PM Monday to Thursday and 11 PM Friday and Saturday. Moore: until 11 PM Monday to Thursday and midnight Friday and Saturday. Sunday both stores run 11 AM to 8 PM.

Is the store accessible?

Yes. Both stores have a wheelchair-accessible entrance and accessible parking.

Can I check if something is in stock?

Call the store and ask. Norman is (405) 217-2330 and Moore is (405) 735-7677.
